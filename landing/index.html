Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser storage (IndexedDB) on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

What if I change my phone?

Export a backup file in Settings and import it on the new device. It takes a couple of taps.

Is it really free?

Yes. No plans, no ads, no in-app purchases. The code is open source.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first launch the app works fully offline.

Which languages are supported?

English and Russian — switch any time in Settings.
